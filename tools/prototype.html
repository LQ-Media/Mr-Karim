Catching a duplicate

Type a relative the way a cousin might enter them. A Malay name states its owner’s father — bin Ismail means son of Ismail — so the app can recognise someone already in the tree before a second copy of them is ever created.

This is a prototype running entirely in your browser. It uses the same kinship and matching code as the real app, with a made-up family of 21 people across four generations. Nothing here is saved.
